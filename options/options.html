    About & help
    What this extension does, and how to reach me.
    What it is A summarizer for YouTube. On any video that has captions, it adds a panel that turns the transcript into a clean, timestamped summary — so you can read the gist in a minute or jump straight to the part you care about.
    How it works It pulls the video's transcript, sends it to the AI provider you've set up (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or your own endpoint — always with your own key), and renders the result as expandable sections — each with a timestamp that seeks the player when you click it.
    Detail & theme Dial each summary from a quick skim to a thorough breakdown with the Detail control in the panel header. The panel follows YouTube's light or dark theme on its own — override it any time under Appearance.
    What is sent where Your API keys, preferences and stats live only in this browser's local storage, never on a server of mine — and only the extension's own pages can read them, not the YouTube page. The transcript is sent to the provider that generates the summary, and nowhere else. On Auto that provider is chosen from the ones you have configured, and if the first one fails the transcript is sent to the next — members-only videos included. Keys being local is not the same as local AI: summarizing runs on the provider's servers unless you have pointed a custom provider at a local endpoint.
    Captions When YouTube's transcript panel is unavailable, the extension reads the caption track the player already loaded for the video you are watching. Those caption responses stay in that page's memory — at most four tracks, cleared as soon as you navigate — and go nowhere except to your chosen provider, as the transcript.
    Site access The extension can reach youtube.com and the three built-in provider APIs, and nothing else. Adding a custom provider asks Chrome for access to that one endpoint, and deleting the provider hands it back. Remote endpoints must use https — plain http is allowed only for localhost.
